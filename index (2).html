Reimbursement payment

Open Google Pay Open any UPI app (prefilled)

If the app opens to its home screen or does not prefill the details (common with Google Pay for personal UPI IDs): go back to Slack, open the Employee UPI QR image, scan it in Google Pay, enter the amount shown above, and pay.
